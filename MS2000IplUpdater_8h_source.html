16/**
17 * Korg MS2000 / MS2000R Hardware Firmware Updater & 1 MB Flash Backup ("IPL") Protocol.
18 *
19 * Implements the host-side IPL SysEx protocol (F0 42 23 58 <cmd> ... F7) dissected from:
20 * - Korg MS2000 v1.07 IPL ROM (H8S/2350 @ 0x4070F0, flash offset 0x000D54..0x001DD6)
21 * - Korg Windows System Update Utility vup_ms2000.exe v1.3
22 * - unknown-technologies fwpatch / fwdump (IPL Command 0x01 Flash Dump at RAM 0x408084)
23 *
24 * Strictly for Korg MS2000 and MS2000R (original DSP56362 + H8S/2350 hardware).
25 * NEVER use with MS2000B / MS2000BR (different hardware/firmware).
26 */
