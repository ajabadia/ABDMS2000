Descripción detallada

4-Stage Stereo All-Pass Phaser extracted from P:$105D - $10A7 (r3 = $0260).

Microcode Reference:

  • Parameter block: X:$0260..$027F, Y:$0330..$0335
  • Runs 100% in internal 24-bit X: RAM (no external DRAM used).
  • 4 cascaded 1st-order all-pass stages per channel (P:$106F - $107E Left, P:$107F - $108F Right).
  • Quadratic-shaped triangle LFO (P:$1090 - $10A6, mpy x0,x0,b at P:$1091) with inverted stereo LFO polarity between Left and Right channels.
