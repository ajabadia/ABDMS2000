Descripción detallada

MS2000 VoxWave Oscillator — formant-based vocal wave synthesis.

The Korg MS2000 VoxWave simulates human vocal sounds by passing a pulse train through 5 resonant bandpass filters tuned to vowel formant frequencies. Control 1 morphs between vowel shapes (A-E-I-O-U).
