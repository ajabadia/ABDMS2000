Descripción detallada

MS2000 Low Frequency Oscillator. LFO1: Saw, Square, Triangle, Sample & Hold. LFO2: Saw, Square+ (random pulse width), Sine, Sample & Hold. Frequency range: logarithmic 0.01 Hz to 20 Hz (exponent ~1.25 per HW measurements). Key Sync: Off (free-run), Timbre (reset on first note of layer), Voice (reset per key press).
