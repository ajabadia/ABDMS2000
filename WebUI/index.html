Results Interpretation Guide:
  • If enabling "Bypass VCF Filter" restores sound: The ZDF filter Cutoff was at 0 or damping was over-attenuating.
  • If enabling "Bypass Envelope / VCA" sounds continuously: EG2 envelope (ADSR) or VCA level was not triggering on notes.
  • If enabling "Bypass OSC1 Mixer" restores sound: OSC1 Mixer volume level was at 0.
  • If enabling "Bypass Mod FX / Delay / EQ" restores sound: One of the effects processors was muting the output stage.
