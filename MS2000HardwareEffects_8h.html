Descripción detallada

Standalone, bit-accurate C++20 reference implementations of all Korg MS2000 DSP56362 hardware effects and voice post-processing blocks, extracted directly from the Motorola DSP56362 microcode (P:$0000..$12DA, X:$0000..$0BFF, Y:$0020..$0BFF) and Hitachi H8S/2350 host driver (x811v107.sys / flash.bin).

All effects on the Korg MS2000 run inside the single Motorola DSP56362 @ 100 MHz (48,000 Hz ESAI sample interrupt P:$03B3), sharing a 1M x 16-bit external EDO DRAM (KM416V1204CT mapped at Y:$400000..$4FFFFF).

This module is intentionally self-contained and decoupled from ABDSharedCode so individual algorithms can be inspected, tested, or migrated independently.
