Descripción detallada

Korg MIDI System Exclusive 7-to-8 bit Unpacker and 8-to-7 bit Packer.

Standard MIDI limits data bytes to 7 bits (0x00..0x7F). Korg hardware groups data in chunks of 7 bytes of 8-bit memory, encoding them into 8 bytes of 7-bit MIDI data with an initial MSB collector byte:

Encoding: [MSB Collector] [Byte0 & 0x7F] [Byte1 & 0x7F] ... [Byte6 & 0x7F] MSB Collector bit i (0..6) = (Byte i bit 7).
